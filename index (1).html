Selected work

Each of these started as a repeated manual task or a blind spot, and became something that runs without a person.

Stuck-transaction requery on Rundeck

RundeckPythonAnsibleSQL

Problem. Stuck VAS transactions had to be found in the database and updated by hand.

What I built. A Rundeck job that scans for stuck transactions and requeries them, with both scheduled and on-demand runs.

Result. Delivered cleanly and running as intended, with less manual effort and fewer processing delays. My lead rated the quarter above expectations.

KBPS requery for maxed-out transactions

AutomationReliability

Problem. Transactions that hit their retry limit needed someone to reprocess them.

What I did. I proposed and oversaw the design and delivery of an automated requery job that detects and reprocesses pending transactions on its own.

Result. Better transaction success rates and fewer escalations.

Settlement visibility and alerting

GrafanaNew RelicPagerDuty

Problem. Settlement failures were found after customers felt them.

What I built. A settlement dashboard for VAS transactions, Airtel “026 count” alerts, and internal settlement success-rate tracking. Alerts use a volume guard so quiet hours don’t trigger false alarms.

Result. Failures are caught earlier, with less alert noise.

Incident discipline, automated

PagerDutyConfluenceSlack

Problem. Post-incident reviews were chased by hand and often late.

What I built. Automated tracking and nudging for P1 post-incident review compliance, plus work on generating postmortems from PagerDuty into Confluence.

Result. Fewer manual follow-ups and clearer accountability after incidents.

Self-service cache clearing

Back OfficeToil reduction

Problem. Routine cache clears needed the infrastructure team.

What I built. A cache-clearing button in the Back Office so support can do it themselves.

Provider incident log

Slack Workflow BuilderGoogle Sheets

Problem. Telco and provider failures weren’t recorded in one place.

What I built. A Slack workflow that logs each provider failure straight into a shared sheet, so patterns are visible when raising issues with providers.
